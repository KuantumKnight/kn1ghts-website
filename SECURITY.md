# Security policy

Thank you for helping protect KN1GHTS.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security concerns privately at https://kn1ghts.org/contact and choose **Security research** as the subject. Include the affected URL or component, a clear reproduction, potential impact, and a way to contact you. Avoid including third-party secrets or personal information.

Please do not publish a report or open a public GitHub issue containing vulnerability details before the team has had the opportunity to investigate.

## Boundaries

The public KN1GHTS site and its administration are **not** blanket-authorized targets for exploit testing. Only designated CTF challenges are intentionally vulnerable, and only within their published competition scope. Do not conduct denial-of-service attacks, compromise other users, access private data, or pivot beyond a challenge sandbox.

## Coordinated disclosure

We aim to acknowledge actionable reports and coordinate fixes, but do not promise a specific response or remediation time. You may contact the team again using the same reporting channel if a report needs follow-up.
